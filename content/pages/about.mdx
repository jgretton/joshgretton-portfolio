## About Me

Hey, I'm Josh, a self-taught front-end developer based in the UK working mostly with React, Next.js and Tailwind CSS. I am currently in the process of learning Laravel through building small projects.

My main experience is through freelance work, designing and building sites for clients.

Outside of coding, volleyball is my thing. I coach, referee and play, so it takes up a fair chunk of my week. It also 100% explains why nearly every project I build ends up volleyball-related, I can't really help myself. The main one is [Volleyscore](/volleyscore), a scoreboard app I designed and built.

### Skills & Technologies

- **Front-End Development:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Development Tools:** Git, GitHub, VS Code, Figma, npm, Vercel
- **CMS & E-commerce:** Shopify Headless, Wix Headless, Sanity
